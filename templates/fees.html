Payment

  • Lesson fees are collected termly and are payable in advance before each term begins.
  • Term fees are based on the actual number of scheduled lessons in each term, and may therefore vary.
  • Refer to Class Schedule for Term Dates.
  • Payment can be made via PayNow or credit card. Credit card payments incur a 3.5% processing fee.

School Holidays

  • Regular weekly lessons pause during the March, June, September and December school holidays.
  • Term-time lessons run until the end of November; there are no regular classes in December.
  • Holiday workshops or special programmes may be offered separately, at an additional charge.
