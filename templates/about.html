About Mrs Aw

Hi, I’m Qi Qi, but most of my students call me Mrs Aw.

I started tutoring at 17, and over the years I have taught at a private international college, written curriculum and trained teachers at an enrichment centre, and worked with students across Primary and Secondary levels.

My classes are built on one simple belief: students do better when they know exactly what to do.

That is why, over the years, I have found myself constantly creating frameworks for the parts of English that students struggle to ‘see’ clearly, from oral responses and situational writing to comprehension, composition and essay writing.

These frameworks are not shortcuts. They are tools that help students think clearly, write purposefully, and respond with confidence under exam conditions.
